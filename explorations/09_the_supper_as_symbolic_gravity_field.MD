## The Supper as a Symbolic Gravity Field

### I. Meaning as Gravitational Force

The Last Supper does not merely organize figures in space.

It organizes **meaning through attraction, curvature, and relational pull**.

In this framework, symbolism behaves like gravity:
- it pulls interpretation toward dense centers  
- it curves perception around focal points  
- it stabilizes unstable regions of meaning  
- it creates orbiting interpretive structures  

The painting is therefore not a flat composition of symbols.

It is a **symbolic gravity field in which meaning is continuously bent by relational intensity**.

### II. Density of Meaning

In physics, gravity emerges from mass.

In Neu-Reconnaissance, symbolic gravity emerges from **meaning density**.

Certain elements in the painting carry higher interpretive mass:
- gesture-laden figures  
- emotionally charged postures  
- central compositional axes  
- narrative-critical moments  

These regions of density:
- attract interpretive attention  
- slow down perception  
- increase cognitive processing load  
- distort surrounding symbolic interpretation  

The painting is not evenly meaningful.

It contains **zones of high symbolic mass that structure the entire field of interpretation**.

In gravitational systems, there exists a boundary beyond which escape is impossible.

Within the symbolic field of the Last Supper, certain meanings approach a similar threshold.
